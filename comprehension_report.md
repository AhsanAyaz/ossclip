**What this proposes**
This design introduces two independent features to `ossclip`: **Caption Themes** (`--caption-theme <preset>`) and **AI Caption Choreography** (`--choreograph-captions`), preceded by a standalone interactive HTML mock page (`packages/scenes/caption-themes-mock.html`) for visual verification prior to Remotion integration. Caption Themes provide five deterministic visual and word-reveal presets (`default`, `founder`, `punch`, `boxed`, `minimal`) that control typography weight/scale, stroke/shadow styles, pill/box backings, and full-line vs. progressive `word-build` reveals without making an LLM call. AI Caption Choreography adds a cached, mechanical-tier LLM pass (`generateCaptionChoreography`) and deterministic validator (`validateCaptionChoreography`) that groups transcript words into source-anchored (`srcStart`) semantic phrases, assigns bounded per-word `emphasisScale` multipliers (`1.0x`–`1.4x`), and tags opening hooks (`role: "hook"`) and sparse punchlines (`role: "impact"`) so `CaptionTrack` can place them in clear vertical safe bands around the speaker's `FaceBox` while preserving phrase grouping and word emphasis across live edits and cuts in `ossclip edit`.

**How the existing system works, per this document**
`ossclip` currently generates burned-in captions by running `buildCaptionLines` in `packages/core/src/captions.ts` to greedily pack kept transcript words into fixed-size lines (`3` words / `1.2s` in 9:16 portrait; `6` words / `2.4s` in 16:9 landscape), flushing at scene `breakpoints` and speech gaps (`maxGap`). In `packages/scenes/src/CaptionTrack.tsx`, `LineView` renders every word in a line at a uniform font size (`64px` portrait, `44px` landscape) with a single hardcoded visual look (weight 900 white text, `10px` black stroke, drop shadow, and active-word color highlight/scale pop), while `captionAnchorForLine` places full-bleed talking-head captions at a fixed vertical anchor (`DEFAULT_CAPTION_ANCHOR = 0.76`) unless overridden by a per-scene `captionY`. In the browser editor (`ossclip edit`), `rebuildCaptionTrack` in `apps/editor/src/liveCaptionTrack.ts` re-runs `buildCaptionLines` deterministically in the browser and layers user overrides (`applyCaptionEdits`, `applyCaptionRangeEdits`, `applyCaptionWordHides`) from `overrides.json` without making network or LLM calls.
